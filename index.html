01 / Research

My work makes visual localization more capable outside the lab: across changing conditions, unknown viewpoints, and imperfect sensors.

01

Trustworthy localization

Systems that know what they do not know—through efficient uncertainty estimation and robust image matching.

02

Spatial representations

Learning place descriptors and neural maps that generalize across viewpoints, time, and modalities.

03

Embodied autonomy

Taking perception into the field, from GNSS-denied flight to the mapping and localization stack of autonomous vehicles.
