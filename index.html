Research

Spatial understanding for autonomous systems

My research brings together computer vision, machine learning, and robotics to help autonomous systems understand and navigate the physical world. I work across autonomous driving and aerial robotics, from visual localization and mapping for self-driving vehicles to night-time drone navigation and landing in GNSS-denied environments.

My interests include spatial understanding, visual localization, representation learning, and reliable perception under changing conditions. I combine algorithm development with experience building datasets, benchmarks, and mapping systems, and testing ideas on real robotic platforms.
