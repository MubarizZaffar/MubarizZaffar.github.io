Postdoctoral Researcher · MAVLab, TU Delft

I develop visual perception and localization methods for autonomous systems operating in complex, changing, and GNSS-denied environments.

Research

Reliable spatial intelligence, grounded in real systems.

At MAVLab, I work on autonomous night-time drone landing on moving ships and wind turbines. I completed my PhD at TU Delft’s Intelligent Vehicles Group, advised by Julian F. P. Kooij, and was a visiting researcher at Carnegie Mellon’s Robotics Institute with Sebastian Scherer.
