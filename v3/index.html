About

I am interested in giving autonomous systems a reliable understanding of space: where they are, what has changed, and when they should doubt an answer.

At MAVLab, I work on autonomous drone landing at night on moving ships and wind turbines in GNSS-denied environments, with the German Aerospace Center (DLR). Before this, I completed a PhD in Computer Vision and Machine Learning at TU Delft's Intelligent Vehicles Group, advised by Julian F. P. Kooij, and was a visiting researcher at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute with Sebastian Scherer.

My research spans learned spatial representations, uncertainty-aware image matching, large-scale mapping, visual place recognition, and fielded autonomous systems. I aim to connect strong learning methods to the realities of robot operation.
