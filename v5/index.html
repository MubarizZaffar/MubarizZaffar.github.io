About

I develop visual perception and localization methods that allow autonomous systems to understand where they are—and how certain they should be—when facing real-world change.

At MAVLab, I work on autonomous drone landing at night on moving ships and wind turbines in GNSS-denied environments. I completed my PhD at TU Delft’s Intelligent Vehicles Group, advised by Julian F. P. Kooij, and was a visiting researcher at Carnegie Mellon University’s Robotics Institute with Sebastian Scherer.
